General

What Mia is, how to invite it, and basic support.

Mia is a Discord bot specialized in Fortnite that lets you manage your account, view your inventory, set up automatic feeds for your server, and much more. It’s designed to make the Fortnite player experience easier directly from Discord.

You can add Mia to your server by clicking the "Install Mia!" button on our home page or clicking here. You only need administrator permissions on the server where you want to add it.

Accounts

Yes: Mia does not ask for or store your Epic password. Registration uses the official authorization flow (device auth) and only stores the technical credentials needed (AccountID, DeviceID, and Secret) in a private database. TaxiBots

Access to STW missions and Battle Royale bot lobbies.

TaxiBots are automated accounts that let you join Save the World (STW) missions at a higher power level than yours. You can also use them tor inter Battle Royale (BR) bot lobbies and play with bots.

To use the TaxiBots system:

  1. Join the Mia Lounge.
  2. Register your account in Mia with /registrar.
  3. Add your account to the taxis with /taxibot (activar).
  4. Add a bot.
  5. Invite a taxi.
  6. Set your match to don’t fill and play!

Full guide on taxibots.
