What is Mia?

Mia is a Discord bot specialized in Fortnite that lets you manage your account, view your inventory, configure automatic feeds for your server, and much more. Through simple commands in Discord, you can access features such as:

  • Viewing your Fortnite locker and cosmetics
  • Managing gifts and purchases from Discord
  • Configuring automatic news and shop feeds
  • Accessing your account statistics and resources
  • Using TaxiBots for automatic missions
  • Accessing the Mia API for developers
