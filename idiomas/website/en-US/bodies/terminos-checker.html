What is Mia Checker?

Mia Checker is a Telegram bot that lets you verify and analyze Fortnite account information quickly and securely. Through simple commands, you can access data such as:

  • Exclusive items and cosmetics from your account
  • Save The World (STW) resources
  • General account statistics
  • Generation of professional sales messages
  • Instant verification without needing to log in to the game

Use of the service via Telegram

Mia Checker operates exclusively through Telegram. To use the service:

  • You must have an active Telegram account.
  • You must accept the Telegram Terms of Service.
  • You must provide the credentials needed to verify your Fortnite account (depending on the command used).
  • You must follow the bot's instructions and not abuse the service.

Privacy and data we process

Mia Checker temporarily processes certain data to provide its features. We do not permanently store your login credentials. The data we process includes:

  • Your Telegram user ID
  • Temporary Epic Games authentication tokens (access tokens)
  • Public information from your Fortnite account (username, items, resources, etc.)
  • Commands executed and usage dates (for analysis and service improvement)

We do not sell, share, or distribute your personal information to third parties. All processed information is used exclusively for the operation of the bot and is deleted after completing the requested operations.
